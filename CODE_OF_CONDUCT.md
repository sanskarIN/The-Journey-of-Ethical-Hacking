# Code of Conduct

We want this repository to remain respectful, educational, and safe for learners and contributors.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Protect privacy and confidential information.
- Use security knowledge only in lawful, authorized contexts.
- Help beginners understand both technical ideas and their safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, doxxing, sharing private credentials or personal information, encouraging unauthorized access, or using the project to target real people or organizations is not acceptable.

## Reporting

Concerns may be reported privately to **sanskarin@outlook.in**.
