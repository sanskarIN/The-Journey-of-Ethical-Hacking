# Endpoint Governance Tabletop — Fictional Exercise

[![Get the Book on Gumroad](https://img.shields.io/badge/Get%20the%20Book-Gumroad-FF90E8?logo=gumroad&logoColor=000000)](https://ramsandesh.gumroad.com)

This exercise is fictional and discussion-only. It requires no access to real endpoints, mobile devices, management consoles, or user accounts.

## Scenario

A fictional organization reviews its mixed fleet of laptops, mobile devices, kiosks, and field tablets. The review finds uneven enrollment status, aging patch evidence, inconsistent telemetry freshness, and several support-lifecycle exceptions.

## Objectives

- Prioritize fictional fleet-review gaps using criticality and evidence age.
- Clarify ownership for enrollment, patch, telemetry, and support-lifecycle evidence.
- Discuss privacy-aware device governance.
- Define authorized escalation and exception-review pathways.
- Plan recovery and replacement readiness at a policy level.

## Discussion prompts

1. Which fictional device classes need the fastest assurance review and why?
2. What evidence shows that enrollment and policy ownership are current?
3. How should patch-age exceptions be reviewed without exposing device secrets or user data?
4. What telemetry-health evidence is sufficient for governance reporting?
5. How should unsupported-device risk be communicated to service owners?
6. What privacy boundaries should apply to endpoint telemetry?
7. What recovery evidence should be retained after a fictional device replacement exercise?

## Outputs

- Fictional fleet assurance register.
- Patch and support exception schedule.
- Telemetry-health review checklist.
- Privacy-aware evidence plan.
- Recovery-readiness summary.

## Safety boundary

Do not use this exercise to unlock, track, inspect, remotely control, erase, bypass, alter, or interfere with real devices, management controls, or user accounts.
