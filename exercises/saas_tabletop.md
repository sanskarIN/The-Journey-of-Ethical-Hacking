# SaaS Governance Tabletop — Fictional Exercise

This exercise is fictional and discussion-only. It does not require logging in to, probing, modifying, or testing any real SaaS tenant.

## Scenario

A fictional enterprise has several collaboration and business SaaS services. A governance review finds uneven administrator reviews, inconsistent external-sharing policy ownership, and different logging and backup/export expectations across service classes.

## Objectives

- Define service ownership and risk-based review frequency.
- Discuss administrator-governance and evidence freshness.
- Review external-sharing and integration governance at a policy level.
- Identify resilience expectations for business-critical SaaS services.
- Improve tenant-governance reporting without exposing sensitive tenant data.

## Discussion prompts

1. Which SaaS services need the strongest assurance cadence?
2. Who should own administrator-review evidence?
3. How should external-sharing exceptions be documented and time-bounded?
4. What resilience evidence matters for business-critical SaaS services?
5. How should integration ownership and offboarding be governed?
6. What metrics show policy drift without over-collecting user activity data?
7. What evidence should be retained for an audit-ready review?

## Outputs

- Fictional SaaS governance matrix.
- Administrator-review schedule.
- External-sharing exception register.
- Resilience evidence checklist.
- Executive summary of fictional assurance gaps.

## Safety boundary

Do not use this exercise to access, enumerate, alter, bypass, or interfere with real SaaS accounts, sessions, tenants, integrations, or user data.
