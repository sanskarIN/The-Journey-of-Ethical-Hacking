# Workforce Security Governance Tabletop — Fictional Exercise

[![Get the Book on Gumroad](https://img.shields.io/badge/Get%20the%20Book-Gumroad-FF90E8?logo=gumroad&logoColor=000000)](https://ramsandesh.gumroad.com)

This exercise is fictional and discussion-only. It does not involve monitoring real employees, collecting personal information, or evaluating individuals.

## Scenario

A fictional security organization is reviewing role coverage, training freshness, exercise participation, succession readiness, and development-plan consistency across several security role families.

## Objectives

- Identify capability gaps without ranking or profiling real people.
- Discuss role coverage, succession, and backup responsibilities.
- Plan training and exercise refresh cycles.
- Define fair, privacy-aware capability evidence.
- Improve leadership reporting on organizational resilience.

## Discussion prompts

1. Which fictional role families have the greatest continuity risk?
2. What evidence demonstrates current training without exposing personal details?
3. How should backup coverage be documented for critical responsibilities?
4. What indicators show that a development plan is active and useful?
5. How should leadership distinguish staffing risk from individual performance?
6. What exercise cadence is appropriate for high-criticality roles?
7. How can workforce metrics avoid creating unhealthy incentives or intrusive monitoring?

## Outputs

- Fictional role-coverage matrix.
- Training refresh calendar.
- Succession and backup-coverage checklist.
- Capability evidence guide.
- Leadership summary focused on roles and systems, not individuals.

## Safety boundary

Use fictional role identifiers only. Do not use this exercise to profile, surveil, rank, pressure, or expose real workers or students.
