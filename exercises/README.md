These exercises are designed for discussion, planning, evidence review, and defensive decision-making. They use fictional organizations and require no live system interaction.

## Included packs

- `resilience_tabletop.md` — continuity, recovery, communications, and decision logging.
- `governance_tabletop.md` — policy exceptions, evidence freshness, ownership, and executive reporting.
- `incident_coordination_tabletop.md` — major-incident roles, communications, evidence preservation, and recovery verification.
- `privacy_tabletop.md` — minimization, retention, deletion, transfer governance, and privacy evidence.
- `saas_tabletop.md` — SaaS ownership, administrator review, sharing governance, and resilience evidence.
- `endpoint_tabletop.md` — fleet posture, patch/support evidence, telemetry governance, and recovery readiness.
- `workforce_tabletop.md` — role coverage, training freshness, succession readiness, and capability governance.

## Safety boundary

Do not turn these tabletop scenarios into instructions for accessing, disrupting, tracking, bypassing, or exploiting real systems or people. If an organization adapts an exercise internally, it should use its own authorized procedures, legal/privacy guidance, and incident-management policies.
