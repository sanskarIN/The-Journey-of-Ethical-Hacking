## Link policy

- Prefer the direct Gumroad storefront URL over URL shorteners.
- Keep the visible text descriptive for accessibility.
- Do not replace the storefront link with X/Twitter or another social-profile URL.
- The public GitHub repository remains a companion-resource project; paid book files are not committed here.
- No author avatar/photo/person image is required for this storefront promotion.
