# Lab Safety Rules

Companion exercises must use only fictional, local, isolated, supplied, or explicitly authorized environments.

## Required rules

- Never place real credentials, API keys, private keys, production secrets, or personal data into an exercise.
- Never target third-party systems, accounts, networks, applications, or devices without explicit authorization.
- Prefer synthetic datasets and read-only evidence review.
- Keep exercises reversible and non-destructive.
- Document the learning objective, scope, and stop conditions.
- Do not include stealth/evasion, malware, credential theft, destructive actions, or bypass instructions.
- Remove sensitive evidence before sharing screenshots or reports.

The goal of the lab material is defensive reasoning, control validation, prioritization, documentation, and recovery readiness.
