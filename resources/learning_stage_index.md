# Learning Stage Index

The 200-part series can be navigated in 20 broad defensive-learning stages.

1. Parts 1–10 — foundations, ethics, systems, networking, assessment methodology, and vulnerability-management basics.
2. Parts 11–20 — secure web, APIs, identity, cryptography, cloud, containers, DevSecOps, and mobile foundations.
3. Parts 21–30 — Android, iOS, desktop/browser security, servers, databases, zero trust, wireless, and endpoint defense.
4. Parts 31–40 — monitoring, SIEM, incident response, forensics, hunting, SOAR, intelligence, exposure, cloud posture, and service trust.
5. Parts 41–50 — software supply chain, secrets/PKI, identity governance, privacy, threat modeling, validation, GRC, resilience, and security-program maturity.
6. Parts 51–60 — advanced enterprise defensive engineering and operational assurance.
7. Parts 61–70 — detection, response, resilience, and security-program integration.
8. Parts 71–80 — advanced assurance, cloud-native, application, identity, and data-protection themes.
9. Parts 81–90 — enterprise monitoring, exposure management, threat hunting, and operational maturity.
10. Parts 91–100 — hunting-program architecture, detection engineering, purple-team/control validation, exposure management, and resilience.
11. Parts 101–110 — product security, software security operations, and integrated security engineering.
12. Parts 111–120 — AI/ML security, cryptographic agility, PKI, machine identity, and trust infrastructure.
13. Parts 121–130 — cloud-native/platform security and zero-trust/identity operating models.
14. Parts 131–140 — enterprise data security, privacy engineering, OT/ICS, cyber-physical resilience, and maturity.
15. Parts 141–150 — IoT/edge security and mobile/endpoint/device governance.
16. Parts 151–160 — collaboration/SaaS security and human-centered/social-engineering defense.
17. Parts 161–170 — GRC, board oversight, cybersecurity strategy, transformation, and investment governance.
18. Parts 171–180 — workforce/talent leadership, organizational learning, research, and emerging-technology readiness.
19. Parts 181–190 — cyber-risk economics, insurance, legal/ethical governance, privacy, investigations, and cross-border assurance.
20. Parts 191–200 — critical-service resilience, ecosystem coordination, enterprise mastery, professional practice, and final integration.
