## Status definitions

- **Reported** — submitted and awaiting verification.
- **Confirmed** — verified against the publication source.
- **Scheduled** — accepted for a future publication update.
- **Corrected** — incorporated into the maintained publication source.
- **Not an error** — reviewed and determined to be intentional/correct.

## What to include in a report

Provide the part number, visible section/topic heading, edition, a short description of the issue, and a proposed correction if known. Do **not** paste long passages from the paid book into a public issue.
